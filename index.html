Built around the craft, not the clock.

Hair City opened in the warehouse district in 2008 on a simple idea: a great salon should feel like a ritual, not an appointment. Today four studios share one roof — hair, nails, spa, and barbering — each led by artists who trained for years to make an hour in the chair feel unhurried.

We keep the crew small enough to know your name and skilled enough to trust with anything from a first fade to a wedding-day updo.
